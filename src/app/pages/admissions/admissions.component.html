Student Welfare & Facilitation

Advertised Financial Assistance & Welfare Schemes

Institutional admission materials advertise student welfare assistance for eligible candidates. The following summaries are presented for candidate information, subject to statutory guidelines and regulatory confirmation.

Advertised Collateral Claim [Subject to Eligibility]
₹4,000 / Month Internship Scholarship

Clinical Internship Stipend Assistance

Institutional admission posters advertise monthly scholarship assistance of ₹4,000 for trainees during mandatory clinical hospital postings and clinical internships.

Qualifying Conditions: Subject to candidate attendance, satisfactory clinical bedside evaluation, formal institutional gazette schedules, and hospital rotational compliance. Not an unconditional payment guarantee.
State Government Scheme [Govt. of Bihar]
Up to ₹4 Lakh Education Loan Facilitation

Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) Assistance

Admission materials reference facilitation support under the Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) Scheme, which assists domicile students with education financing up to ₹4,00,000 for approved programs.

Qualifying Conditions: Loan sanction, interest subsidies, and disbursement are strictly governed by the Department of Education (Govt. of Bihar). Institutional accreditation under the BSCC portal remains subject to regulatory completion.
