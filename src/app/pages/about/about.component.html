Who We Are

Academic Discipline, Compassionate Care

Kavya Shree Institute of Nursing is an educational institution located at Punpun Station Road, opposite Manglam Marriage Hall, PIN 804453, Bihar. The institute advertises nursing education spanning diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels.

The proposed educational framework combines diagnostic discipline with compassionate bedside ethics through classroom coursework, simulation-lab training, and clinical hospital postings.

Five nursing disciplines are advertised for the 2026 academic term: ANM, GNM, B.Sc. Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing, and M.Sc. Nursing. All program offerings remain provisional pending statutory council notifications and official university affiliation.

Administrative Transparency: Statutory university affiliation orders, state nursing council registrations, and clinical hospital tie-up notifications will be updated on this portal upon regulatory completion. No premature regulatory affiliation is asserted.

Educational Framework

Proposed Institutional Vision

The following educational principles represent the proposed instructional direction. Implementation details remain subject to formal regulatory and institutional approvals.

Academic Rigor

Proposed curriculum design combining foundational nursing sciences with clinical reasoning, pharmacology, and community healthcare practices.

Compassionate Care

Cultivating patient dignity, attentive bedside etiquette, family counseling skills, and compassionate healthcare advocacy.

Clinical Competency

Proposed hands-on training through simulation labs, hospital bedside rotations, and supervised clinical internships, pending formal confirmation.
